## Acceptance and stop conditions

M5 passes only with an exact source-state copy, signed source and target, successful private restore and migration, controller or signed-boundary receipts, authenticated postflight, unchanged workload identities/routes, and a demonstrated allowed rollback or signed forward recovery. A fresh v3-only copy, passing unit tests, current healthy Mini, or the completed live M9 Mini adoption cannot alone close M5.

Stop before any effect if source proof, signature, identity baseline, private isolation, or owner-only file checks fail. Stop after a controller failure and preserve its journal; reconcile the exact operation before retrying. Seven queued Fleet GitHub dispatch receipts were present on 2026-11-02. The ordinary direct Mini upgrade can retain these records because its migrator does not require full control-database quiescence; the separate fenced `legacy-baseline` lane requires zero queued receipts. `recovered-forward` may preserve queued dispatches only with its signed transition evidence. Reclassify them at execution rather than assuming today's queue remains unchanged.
